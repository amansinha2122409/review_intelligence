SUPPORT TEAM ACTION:
Please use this approved snippet:

• The Platform Maintenance Fee is an annual administrative charge of ₹250 levied to support account security, regulatory compliance, and cloud infrastructure. • The fee is debited directly from the user's account wallet on an annual basis following account activation. • The levy applies to all registered accounts regardless of trading activity or transaction frequency. • Invoices and transaction receipts detailing the charge are accessible under Account Settings > Billing & Statements. • Standard notifications are issued per regulatory guidelines to the registered email address. • Last checked: October 24, 2023.
