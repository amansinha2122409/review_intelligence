
## Weekly Product Pulse - 2026-10-12
**Identified Fee Issue:** 250 Rs Platform Maintenance Fee

### The Pulse
Summary: Customer sentiment is primarily weighed down by surprise platform maintenance fees, technical instability in the portfolio tab, and support latency. Quotes: 'Why was I charged a 250 Rs Platform Maintenance Fee?', 'App keeps crashing when I try to open the portfolio tab.', and 'Hidden charges! I saw a maintenance fee deducted from my wallet without any notification.' Key Observation: The primary driver of churn and 1-star ratings is a lack of upfront transparency regarding recurring account fees, creating a sense of broken trust exacerbated by unresponsive support during deductions. Action Ideas: 1. Deploy explicit fee disclosure during onboarding and send an advance push/email alert 7 days prior to any maintenance fee debit. 2. Prioritize a hotfix for portfolio tab memory leaks and optimize backend API latency. 3. Implement an in-app billing breakdown and automated fee refund/dispute workflow to decrease customer support queues.
